Put your 24 product images in this folder (public/products/).

Naming: lowercase, hyphenated, matching the keys in lib/productImages.js
Examples:
  car-vacuum.png
  mini-printer.png
  label-printer.png

Square-ish images (e.g. 200x200) look best in the timeline circle.
After adding them, update lib/productImages.js so each product name
keyword maps to its image file.
